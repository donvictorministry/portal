About Rev. Don Victor, PhD

Dr. Don Victor is an ordained Reverend Minister of Jesus Christ, a prophet, multifaceted personality,researcher, trainer, web developer, and software engineer whose work integrates faith, scholarship, technology, for global impact. His academic journey spans foundational WAEC qualification to a PhD in Education, giving him a broad foundation for addressing complex spiritual, educational, technological, and organizational challenges.

Core Focus: Driven by a vision to reach six million souls, Dr. Don Victor has established and developed diverse online and offline ministries, educational initiatives, and technology-driven solutions. His expertise in software engineering enables him to research, design, and deploy practical digital tools that streamline operations, automate processes, support online engagement, deliver education, and advance institutional growth.

As an innovator, he translates scholarly and ministry frameworks into scalable systems and platforms for spiritual growth, Christian education, leadership development, and ministry operations. Through his global initiatives, he facilitates collaboration across diverse networks and builds bridges between faith, education, research, and modern technology to bring truth, healing, and transformation to nations and sphere of society.

September 23, 2026 Birthday Message

Today, as I celebrate another year of life, I do so with profound gratitude to God and sincere appreciation for the countless individuals across the world whom God has strategically used at different stages of my journey.

My life has become a living testimony of grace, intellectual growth, resilience, and divine providence. I have been blessed across many dimensions of life, and I have every reason to acknowledge the faithfulness of God, whose grace has continually sustained and advanced me.

Years ago, I began my ministry journey with little more than an incomplete Bible and the ability to pray. I carried a deep passion for my calling as an evangelist and prophet, and a firm conviction that my life would make a meaningful contribution to humanity. Today, by the grace of God, I have had the privilege of advancing from those modest beginnings into the academic world, earning a Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) and becoming increasingly engaged in scholarship, research, education, ministry, and technological innovation.

Along the way, my work and contributions have been recognised across different nations as those of a global multifaceted personality from Africa. When I reflect upon this journey, I realise that no single narrative can adequately capture the magnitude of God’s dealings with my life, the opportunities, lessons, relationships, breakthroughs, and accomplishments that have shaped my journey. There is simply too much to recount.

Key Highlight: Therefore, on this special day, I do not want to celebrate myself, but rather celebrate the Giver of life and the grace of God that has brought me this far. All glory, all honour, and all praise belong to the King of kings and Lord of lords.

Join me in celebrating the goodness, faithfulness, and extraordinary grace of God that have shaped and sustained my journey. Adonai, ani moser et atzmi lekha. Todah lekha.

Jesus Christ is Lord forever

Dr. Don Victor
